To protect a business scenario, reuse reviewed Policies before composing, validating, and publishing a Guardrail. The current console requires administrator access to create and publish; this section groups operational tasks rather than granting write access to every sign-in role.

1. **Browse Policy Library** — Review purpose, Rules, parameters, and Test Cases.

2. **Create a Guardrail** — Describe business boundaries and select Policy Versions, Rules, and Rails.

3. **Run Validation** — Execute the current draft's Test Cases through the production-equivalent NeMo runtime.

4. **Publish** — Freeze the passing draft as an immutable Guardrail Version.

5. **Connect business traffic** — Prepare an Endpoint, target the pinned version from a Router Route, publish, and confirm that Runners applied the target configuration.

6. **Review and improve** — Try the behavior in Playground and use runtime and audit evidence to plan the next policy version.
