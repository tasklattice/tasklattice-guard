### How one request is protected

This is the main path from product objects to runtime execution. Every transition has a version and audit boundary.

<FlowSteps>

<FlowStep title="Endpoint">

Identifies and authenticates the application, agent, or gateway calling TaskLattice.

</FlowStep>

<FlowStep title="Router">

Matches ordered Routes in the bound Router, then selects a weighted Target pinned to a Guardrail Version.

</FlowStep>

<FlowStep title="Guardrail Version">

Pins Policies, Rules, parameters, and compiled output as the release and rollback unit.

</FlowStep>

<FlowStep title="NeMo Rail / Flow">

Orchestrates checks before the model or before its output is delivered.

</FlowStep>

<FlowStep title="Action">

Runs local checks, specialized evaluators, or content transformations.

</FlowStep>

<FlowStep title="Decision & Evidence">

Returns allow, transform, or block and records privacy-conscious evidence.

</FlowStep>

</FlowSteps>
