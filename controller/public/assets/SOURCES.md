# Asset sources

- `brand/tasklattice-mark.svg` and `/favicon.svg` — unchanged copies of the TaskLattice website's `public/assets/brand/tasklattice-mark.svg`, also used by Veritas. The inline `BrandMark` component reuses the website's `app/BrandLogo.tsx` geometry and brand signal colors; product names use one shared wordmark layout.
- `models/deepseek.svg` — DeepSeek brand mark from [Simple Icons v16](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), distributed under CC0-1.0. Brand use remains subject to the owner's trademark terms.
- `providers/deepseek.webp`, `nvidia.webp`, `ollama.webp`, `openai.webp`, `qwen.webp`, and `vllm.webp` — matching Provider avatars copied from TaskLattice Relay's checked-in [Lobe Icons](https://lobehub.com/icons) static-avatar set. The files were originally retrieved from [`lobehub/lobe-icons`](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons/tree/master/packages/static-avatar/avatars) on 2026-07-18. Product names and marks remain the property of their respective owners.
- `providers/laya.webp` — Laya logo supplied by the user.
- `providers/custom.svg` — TaskLattice Relay-owned neutral interface artwork for custom OpenAI-compatible endpoints.
